Help & FAQ

Quick answers about how the simulator works

How does progress get saved?

Everything is saved locally in this browser, tied to your account on this computer. It is not synced to any server.

What does "In Progress" vs "Completed" mean?

An exercise becomes In Progress the moment you start filling it in, and Completed once you finish every required step and reach the final review.

Where do I continue an exercise I started?

Open My Tasks in the sidebar, or the Document Library on the main Dashboard — both link straight back to where you left off.

Can I reset my progress?

Yes — under Account Settings there is a "Reset All My Progress" option. This cannot be undone.
